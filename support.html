Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Email akshat0120@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How to play

  • Tap PLAY! and choose a stage. Stage 1 is unlocked first, and clearing a stage unlocks the next one.
  • Allow camera access, then look around with your phone to find the bombs.
  • Point the centre crosshair at a bomb and tap anywhere on the screen to fire.
  • Clear every bomb before the timer or your ammo runs out.

Common questions

I can’t find the bombs. Turn around slowly in a well-lit room. Bombs can appear on any side of you, a few metres away.

The screen is black. The app needs camera access. Go to Settings → AR Bomb Squad on your iPhone and turn on Camera.

There’s no sound. Check that the silent switch is off and that sound is on in the app’s Settings.

How do I start over? In the app, go to Settings → Reset Levels.

Which devices are supported? Any iPhone that supports ARKit and runs iOS 15 or later.
