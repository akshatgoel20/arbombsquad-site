AR Bomb Squad – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

AR Bomb Squad (“the app”) is a single-player augmented reality game for iPhone, made by Akshat Goel. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Camera

The app uses your device’s camera only to show the augmented reality game and place bombs in the room around you. Apple’s ARKit processes camera images on your device in real time. The app never records, saves or sends them anywhere.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your sound settings (sound on or off, and volume), the stages you’ve unlocked, and your best score for each stage. This information stays on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. To delete it, use Settings → Reset Levels in the app, or delete the app.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
