AR Bomb Squad – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

AR Bomb Squad (“the app”) is a single-player augmented reality game for iPhone, made by Akshat Goel. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Summary

  • No accounts, no names or email addresses, no ads and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • Camera images never leave your device.
  • If you allow it, the app sends anonymous gameplay statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase so we can see which stages are too hard or too easy and fix problems. You can turn this off any time.

Anonymous usage statistics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service from Google, to collect:

  • Gameplay events: when you start and finish a stage, whether you cleared it or ran out of time or ammo, your score, play time, shots and hits, time until your first hit, how well augmented reality tracking worked, quitting a stage, changes to settings, and the furthest stage you’ve unlocked.
  • App usage: app opens, screens viewed, and how long and how often the app is used.
  • Device and app information: device model, iOS version, app version, language and approximate country or region. Google works out the region from your internet connection; we never see your IP address.
  • A random app-instance ID created by Firebase, so events from the same install can be counted together. It isn’t linked to your name, Apple ID or advertising identifier, and it changes if you delete and reinstall the app.

We don’t collect your name, email, contacts, photos, camera images, precise location or advertising identifier (IDFA), and we don’t use this data for advertising, sell it, or combine it with data from other companies to track you.

We use these statistics only to understand how the game is played and to improve it. Detailed event data is kept by Google for up to 2 months; summary reports (for example, “60% of players cleared Stage 3”) may be kept longer. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy; see also how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

New stages

To get new weekly stages, the app downloads stage files from our website (akshatgoel20.github.io, hosted by GitHub). These downloads don’t include any information about you, and they happen whether or not usage statistics are switched on. Like any website, GitHub’s servers see your IP address in order to deliver the files; we don’t receive or keep it.

Your choices

Turn statistics on or off any time in the app under Settings → Privacy → Share Usage Data. When it’s off, nothing is sent. If your device’s region is in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks before sending anything, and nothing is sent unless you choose “Share”. Elsewhere it’s on unless you turn it off.

Because the statistics aren’t linked to your identity, we can’t look up or single out your data. To stop collection, turn the setting off; deleting the app also resets its random ID. For any question about your data, contact us below.

Camera

The app uses your device’s camera only to show the augmented reality game and place bombs in the room around you. Apple’s ARKit processes camera images on your device in real time. The app never records, saves or sends them anywhere. Whether you allowed camera access is included in the statistics above, but no images are.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your sound settings (sound on or off, and volume), the stages you’ve unlocked, your best score for each stage, and your statistics choice on your device. To delete it, use Settings → Reset Levels in the app, or delete the app.

Children

The app is rated 13+ and isn’t directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
